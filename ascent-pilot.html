Pilot design

Measure readiness patterns without changing how the operation runs.

The Ascent pilot is designed as a six-week observational study. It does not rank vendors, assess fitness for duty or add pad paperwork. It asks a management question: where does measurable readiness deterioration begin across shifts and rotations?
