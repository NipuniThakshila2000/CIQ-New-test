Predict the readiness of your crew.

FRMS fit

Why it matters

Fatigue systems predict the average roster. CIQ checks the real crew member.

Duty-time limits and biomathematical models are useful, but they do not know how a specific pilot, controller or maintenance team member is doing before duty. CIQ adds a measured readiness signal that can calibrate those systems without exposing individual scores.
