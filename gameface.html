GameFace athlete readiness

Read the athlete before the moment decides.

GameFace turns a short face-forward check-in into practical readiness context for performance, pressure and recovery.

01

Face-forward

A fast, low-friction reading captures visible state before training, competition or return-to-play work.

02

Pressure aware

Gaze, voice and self-report context help separate focused intensity from accumulating strain.

03

Team ready

Coaches and support staff see only consented, useful signals that help guide load and recovery.

Performance support

Elite preparation depends on the state the athlete brings into the session.

The GameFace video frames readiness through close facial state and team performance moments. CIQ uses that idea as a practical layer for athletes: a private check-in for the person, and controlled, contextual insight for the people responsible for preparation and recovery.

PreCheck readiness before training, selection, competition or high-pressure review.
PostTrack recovery state after travel, match load, injury work or intense sessions.
ShareLet the athlete decide what reaches coaches, clinicians or performance staff.
