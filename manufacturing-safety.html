Manufacturing operator safety

Turn invisible fatigue into an early signal.

CIQ helps safety leaders see shift-level readiness before fatigue reaches the machine.

01

Private

Each readiness report belongs to the operator and is not exposed to supervisors.

02

Aggregated

Leaders see anonymized readiness by shift, line or team, enough to rebalance work.

03

Preventive

Use fatigue as a leading indicator beside existing safety systems.

Hazard mitigation

A fatigued operator can look present while performance is already degraded.

The manufacturing brief frames fatigue as an invisible cause behind visible workplace injury, cost and disruption. CIQ gives operators a private readiness check while supervisors receive only anonymized shift, line or team-level indicators.
